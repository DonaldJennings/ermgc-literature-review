\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Search Strategy}
Citation tracking was chosen as the primary method because the relevant work
is judged on implementation detail that keyword searches cannot select for,
and it is scattered across IEEE, MDPI and SPIE venues, university theses and
adjacent fields \cite{wohlinGuidelinesSnowballingSystematic2014}.
The corpus was built in two stages.
First, on 30~October~2026, three seed papers
\cite{zhaoGPUAcceleratedSignalProcessing2023,
  bensonGPUbasedSpacetimeAdaptive2013a, perdanaSurveyGraphicsProcessing2017}
had their reference lists followed backwards and their citing and same-author
work followed forwards.
This was supplemented by targeted searches on the themes raised in
Section~\ref{sec:intro}: data movement, execution models, auto-tuning, reduced
precision and timing measurement.
Adjacent domains with mature GPU streaming practice, chiefly radio astronomy,
were included where they hold the strongest evidence for operations radar
shares.
Second, to check the coverage of the first stage, two strings were run on
\searchdate{} across all metadata in IEEE Xplore and across abstracts in the
ACM Digital Library:
\begin{itemize}
  \item (GPU OR CUDA OR GPGPU) AND (radar OR ``synthetic aperture'' OR STAP
        OR CFAR OR ``pulse compression'' OR beamforming)
  \item (GPU OR CUDA) AND (roofline OR benchmarking OR ``unified memory''
        OR ``CUDA Graphs'' OR auto-tuning)
\end{itemize}
These returned \nXploreA{} and \nXploreB{} records in IEEE Xplore and
\nACMA{} and \nACMB{} in the ACM Digital Library.
For the first string, the 100 most relevant records in IEEE Xplore and all
\nACMA{} in the ACM Digital Library were screened by title and abstract
(\nScreened{} in total), adding \nAdded{} implementation studies not found in
the first stage \cite{perdanaRadarSignalProcessing2019,
  pavlovImplementationSyntheticAperture2019,
  fangRealtimeImplementationPhased2024} (Fig.~\ref{fig:search-flow}).
A fourth relevant study had already been identified by citation tracking
\cite{yangGPUOrientedDesignsConstant2022a}.
The second string, on performance method, was not screened: the method
literature this review relies on is a small set of established works reached
directly from the seeds and from each other, so the check was aimed at the
implementation studies that the appraisal depends on.
Citation tracking can miss work outside the seeds' citation network; one
addition shares an author with a seed paper yet was not reached by author
tracking.
The small number of additions suggests the first stage captured most of the
radar implementation literature, though not that it is exhaustive, and
coverage of performance-method work was not independently checked.

\subsection{Selection}
Studies were included if they (i)~implement or measure a radar or sensor signal processing stage on a GPU, or supply a high-performance computing method used to judge such work; (ii)~report quantitative performance; and (iii)~are peer-reviewed or, in the case of a thesis or technical report, contain a clear methodology. Issues surrounding certification, fault tolerance, real-time scheduling theory and performance portability were excluded as out of scope. Of the \nStageOne{} items identified by citation tracking and the \nAdded{} added by the coverage check, \nIncluded{} are included in this review, spanning 1995 to 2026, with 25 published in 2016 or later.

\subsection{Appraisal}
Each of the \nAppraised{} implementation studies was read in full and recorded in an
evidence matrix against six criteria (Table~\ref{tab:rubric}).
Three follow Hoefler and Belli's rules for interpretable benchmarking: the CPU
baseline, since a speed-up means little without the absolute performance of
its base case; the depth of analysis, since bounds such as the roofline make a
result interpretable; and the timing statistics, since nondeterministic
measurements need a measure of spread
\cite{hoeflerScientificBenchmarkingParallel2015a}.
Whether transfers are timed follows Gregg and Hazelwood
\cite{greggWhereDataWhy2011a}.
The execution model and energy measurement were added from the review question
itself: the first is an implementation strategy, and the second a cost that is
rarely measured directly.
Cells record only what each paper states; anything unstated was recorded as
unclear rather than inferred.
The review had a single reviewer, so screening and appraisal were not
independently checked; verifying every matrix row against the full text
partly mitigates this.
